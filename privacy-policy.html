Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 6, 2026  |  Last Updated: November 7, 2026

WAZAM is built on a simple principle: the audio you send us is used to identify sounds, then discarded from our servers. The app keeps your most recent recordings on your own device so you can play them back. We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers. We collect only what is necessary to make the app work and improve over time.

1. Who We Are

WAZAM is an independent mobile application developed by Omar Guadalupe Mora Estrada, based in Hayward, California. WAZAM uses artificial intelligence to identify birds and insects from sound recorded through your device's microphone.

For privacy-related questions, contact us at: omoraestrada@outlook.com

2. Information We Collect

Audio Recordings

When you tap the Listen button, WAZAM records audio through your device's microphone. A recording lasts 12 seconds by default; you can change this to 8 or 15 seconds in Settings, tap Extend to lengthen a recording to 15 seconds, or stop early. Recordings are never longer than 15 seconds.

On our servers, your audio is:

  • Transmitted securely (HTTPS) to our servers for processing
  • Analyzed by our AI models (BirdNET for birds, our own model for insects) to identify species
  • Deleted from our servers as soon as processing finishes, usually within seconds
  • Never saved to storage on our servers and never associated with your identity

On your device, the app keeps a copy of your most recent recordings (about 50) so you can replay them from your results and History. These copies stay in the app's own storage on your phone and are not uploaded again. Older recordings are deleted automatically as new ones are added. You can delete them all at any time with Clear History in Settings, or by deleting the app. Like other app data, they may be included in your device's own backups (for example iCloud or Google backups) if you have backups turned on.

Location Data (Optional)

If you grant location permission, WAZAM reads your device's location (latitude and longitude) when you tap Listen, and once during first-time setup, to filter species suggestions to those known to occur in your region. WAZAM only requests location while you are using the app; it never tracks your location in the background.

  • Your coordinates are sent with each identification request. Our server converts them into a region code (for example "US-CA") using OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service, which receives the coordinates for that lookup (see Section 10).
  • For an identification request, we do not save your coordinates in any database. They may appear in our server logs, which are deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • If you submit feedback on an identification ("Was this correct?"), the precise coordinates of that identification are saved with your feedback and retained in our cloud storage (Google Cloud Storage) to improve our models. See "Feedback Data" below.
  • Location is never shared with advertisers.
  • Location is entirely optional: the app works without location access.

Feedback Data

When you answer "Was this correct?" after an identification, we collect:

  • Whether the identification was correct or incorrect, and your correction, if provided
  • The species that was identified, the top three suggestions, and their confidence levels
  • Your detected region code, and your precise location coordinates if you allowed location access
  • The length of the recording and how many segments were analyzed (not the audio itself)
  • The app version and platform (iOS or Android)
  • A random identifier for that identification request

Feedback is used to improve WAZAM's identification accuracy. It is not linked to your name, email address, or an account. Please note that precise coordinates can reveal where you were when you made a recording.

Usage Analytics

For each identification, our server records a short summary: the time, your region code (state or province level, if location was available), the top species identified and its confidence, and how long processing took. These summaries contain no audio, coordinates, IP address, or device identifiers. They help us understand how the app is used and identify technical issues.

Technical Logs

Like most online services, our servers automatically log technical information about each request, such as your IP address, the time, and the address requested (which, for some requests, includes your coordinates). We use these logs for security, to diagnose problems, and to enforce the daily limit on free identifications, which is counted by IP address. Server logs are deleted automatically after 30 days.

Subscriptions (WAZAM Pro)

WAZAM Pro is an optional subscription. It is purchased and billed through your Apple App Store account (or your Google Play account on Android). Apple or Google handles the payment. WAZAM never receives your payment card details, billing address, or store account details.

WAZAM uses RevenueCat, a subscription-management service, to keep track of subscription status. If RevenueCat is enabled in your version of the app:

  • The app creates an anonymous app user ID, a random identifier that isn't linked to your name, email address, or store account. The app sends RevenueCat this ID and your purchase and subscription status (which product you bought, when, and whether it is active), along with technical information that comes with any network request, such as your IP address, platform, app version, and locale.
  • When you identify a sound, the app sends WAZAM's server the same anonymous app user ID. Our server checks that ID with RevenueCat to see whether it has an active WAZAM Pro subscription, and if it does, applies the WAZAM Pro daily limit. For subscribers, that limit is counted by the anonymous app user ID instead of by IP address.
  • Our server doesn't save the anonymous app user ID. It keeps only a one-way hash of it in memory: for a few minutes to remember the result of the check, and for up to a day to count the daily limit. Our logs contain only a shortened version of that hash.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Your name, email address, or account information (no WAZAM account required)
  • Payment card or billing details, or your Apple or Google account details (purchases are handled by Apple or Google)
  • Audio recordings on our servers after processing
  • Your location in the background or when you are not using the app
  • Contacts, photos, or any data unrelated to sound identification
  • Device identifiers used for advertising
  • Browsing history or activity outside WAZAM

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Identify bird and insect species from your audio recordings
  • Filter species suggestions based on your geographic region
  • Check whether you have an active WAZAM Pro subscription, and apply the matching daily identification limit
  • Improve the accuracy of our AI identification models using feedback
  • Diagnose and fix technical problems with the app, and protect our service from abuse
  • Understand general usage patterns to improve the user experience

5. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your data with advertisers. We share limited information only in these circumstances:

  • Service providers: We use Google Cloud to process audio, run our AI models, and store feedback and logs. Google processes data on our behalf under its data processing terms.
  • RevenueCat: When RevenueCat is enabled in the app, the app shares the anonymous app user ID and your purchase and subscription status (if any) with RevenueCat, which manages subscription status on our behalf. This happens whether or not you subscribe. Our server sends RevenueCat only that ID, to check it. No audio, location, or payment details are shared with RevenueCat.
  • Apple and Google: Subscriptions are purchased and billed through the App Store or Google Play under their terms. We don't send them your audio or location.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim: If you allow location access, our server sends your coordinates to Nominatim to convert them into a region code. No audio or other information about you is included.
  • eBird API (Cornell Lab): We send your region code (e.g., "US-CA") to eBird's API to retrieve regional species lists. No personal information is transmitted.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

6. Data Retention

  • Audio recordings on our servers: Deleted as soon as processing finishes (within seconds); never saved
  • Audio recordings on your device: The most recent recordings (about 50) are kept in the app on your phone for playback, until you clear your History or delete the app
  • Location sent with identifications: Not saved, except in server logs, which are deleted after 30 days
  • Location sent with feedback: Retained with that feedback, as below
  • Feedback data: Retained indefinitely to train future models
  • Usage analytics: Retained indefinitely; these summaries contain no audio, coordinates, IP address, or device identifiers
  • Server logs: Deleted automatically after 30 days
  • Subscription status: RevenueCat keeps the subscription record linked to your anonymous app user ID, and Apple or Google keep their purchase records, under their own policies. Our server keeps only a one-way hash of the ID, in memory: for a few minutes for the subscription check, and for up to a day for the daily limit count.

7. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your information:

  • Microphone access: You can revoke microphone permission at any time in your device settings. The app cannot identify sounds without it.
  • Location access: You can revoke location permission at any time. The app continues to work without location, with less precise regional filtering.
  • Recordings on your device: You can delete all saved recordings at any time with Clear History in Settings.
  • Feedback: Feedback is optional; if you don't want your location saved, don't submit feedback, or turn off location access first.
  • Subscriptions: You can manage or cancel WAZAM Pro at any time in your App Store or Google Play account settings. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
  • Data deletion: We do not keep WAZAM accounts or user profiles. If you want feedback you submitted to be deleted, email us with the approximate date, time, and place, and we will delete any records we can match. If you want the subscription record that RevenueCat keeps for your app deleted, email us with your App Store or Google Play order ID so we can find it. Apple and Google keep their own purchase records under their policies.

8. Children's Privacy

WAZAM is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information to us, please contact us at omoraestrada@outlook.com and we will promptly delete it.

9. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

  • All data transmitted between the app and our servers uses HTTPS encryption
  • Audio is processed on our servers and deleted as soon as processing finishes
  • We use Google Cloud's security infrastructure, which meets SOC 2 and ISO 27001 standards
  • Access to our systems is restricted to authorized personnel only

WAZAM uses the following third-party services:

  • RevenueCat: Manages WAZAM Pro subscription status, using an anonymous app user ID. RevenueCat Privacy Policy
